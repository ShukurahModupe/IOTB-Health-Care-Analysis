📌 Key Findings
**Patient Demographics**

Total patients analyzed: 54,966.

Gender distribution is nearly equal (Female: 27,470 | Male: 27,496).

A+ blood group is the most common among patients.

Age group 61+ has the highest admissions (20,172 patients).

**Medical Conditions**

Arthritis is the most common condition (9,218 cases).

Diabetes generates the highest billing amount (~236M).

Asthma patients have the longest average hospital stay (15.68 days).

**Revenue & Billing**

Total hospital revenue: ₦1.404 billion.

Revenue peaked in 2020 (~283M) but declined in 2023 (~279M).

Cigna is the top insurance contributor (~284M).

Elective admissions generated the highest revenue (~473M).

**Admissions**

Admission types are evenly distributed: Elective (18,473), Urgent (18,391), Emergency (18,102).

December consistently shows the highest admissions.

Female patients dominate emergency admissions (9,166 vs. 8,936 male).

**Test Results**

Abnormal test results: 18,437 cases (~33%).

Arthritis is most associated with abnormal results (3,156 cases).

**Medication Usage
**
Most prescribed medications: Lipitor (11,038), Ibuprofen (11,023), Aspirin (10,984).

Ibuprofen generates the highest billing (~283M).

🔎 **Observations**
The hospital’s workload is highest among elderly patients (61+), requiring more geriatric care.

Arthritis and Diabetes are both medically common and financially significant.

Revenue shows a decline in 2023, suggesting possible operational or external challenges.

Insurance contributions are fairly balanced, but Cigna leads slightly.

Female patients dominate emergency admissions, which may indicate gender-specific health risks.

💡 **Recommendations**
Expand geriatric care facilities to handle the 61+ age group.

Invest in diabetes management programs, given its high billing impact.

Monitor Asthma patients closely due to longer hospital stays.

Strengthen partnerships with Cigna and Medicare to stabilize revenue.

Investigate reasons for the 2023 revenue decline and implement corrective measures.

Ensure adequate supply of A+ blood group in the hospital blood bank.

Track Ibuprofen usage to manage costs and prevent over-prescription.
